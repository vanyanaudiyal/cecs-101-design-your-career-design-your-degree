# SMART Goals Activity

Vague intentions do not produce results. This activity moves you from intention to commitment — from "I hope to" into "I will, by when, measured how." You will write a minimum of 5 SMART Goals across academic, career, and personal development categories.

---

## Step 1 — Review Your POV Statement

Reread your POV Statement. Your goals should move you in the direction defined by your POV Statement. If they do not connect, something is misaligned.

Writing my POV Statement was difficult because I realized that I have many different interests and goals, so it's hard to put everything into one direction. I also realized that a lot of my personality has been shaped by how much I have moved throughout my life. ## Step 2 — Brainstorm Goal Areas

Before writing formal SMART goals, brainstorm freely. For each category below, list 3–4 things you want to accomplish in the next 1–4 years. Do not filter yourself in this step — just generate ideas.

**Academic** *(courses, GPA, relationships with faculty, study habits...)*
- Maintain at least a 3.5 GPA during college.
- Develop better study and time-management habits so I do not procrastinate or feel overwhelmed by my coursework.
- Build relationships with at least 2 professors or academic advisors who can help me explore different career paths within technology and AI.
- Explore different areas of computer science and AI to determine what interests me most instead of feeling pressured to choose one path immediately.

**Career** *(internships, skills, certifications, networking...)*
- Create and improve my resume, LinkedIn profile, and portfolio so I am prepared to apply for internships.
- Apply to at least 10 internships or career opportunities related to AI, technology, business, or another area that interests me.
- Complete at least one AI-related project that I can include in my portfolio.
- Gain an internship or relevant work experience before graduating from college.

**Personal Development** *(campus involvement, health, community, leadership...)*
- Move my body for at least 1 hour daily, alternating between cardio, weights, light walking, and yoga.
- Be kinder to myself and reduce the amount of time I spend comparing my progress to other people.
- Become more comfortable with uncertainty by trying new experiences and opportunities even when I am unsure of the outcome.
- Get more involved on campus and meet people outside of my immediate friend group (that make me feel loved not uncomfortable or test me).

## Step 3 — Select and Write 5 SMART Goals

Choose at least 5 goals from your brainstorm — at minimum, include:
- 2 Academic Goals (at least one short-term and one mid-term)
- 2 Career Goals (at least one short-term and one mid-term)
- 1 Personal Development Goal

For each goal, complete the following SMART evaluation.

### Goal 1 (Academic — Short-Term)

**Goal Statement:**
I will maintain a 3.5 GPA or higher during the current semester by following a consistent study schedule, keeping up with assignments, and preparing for exams in advance.

**S — Specific:** What exactly will I do or achieve?
I will dedicate scheduled time each week to studying, completing assignments, and reviewing material for each of my classes.
**M — Measurable:** How will I track progress? How will I know I succeeded?
I will track my grades throughout the semester and aim to maintain at least a 3.5 semester GPA.

**A — Achievable:** Why is this realistic given my current situation?
This is realistic because I can improve my academic performance through consistent studying, asking for help when needed, and staying organized with my coursework.

**R — Relevant:** How does this connect to my POV Statement and career direction?
Maintaining a strong GPA supports my future career opportunities and helps me build confidence in myself. It also connects to my POV Statement because I want to stop feeling behind and trust that I am capable of reaching my goals.

**T — Time-Bound:** By when will I achieve this? What is my deadline?
I will maintain a 3.5 GPA or higher throughout the Fall 2026 semester.

**Immediate Next Action:** What is one concrete step I can take THIS WEEK toward this goal? 
This week, I will review all of my current grades and upcoming assignments and create a study schedule for the rest of the semester.

### Goal 2 (Academic — Mid-Term)

**Goal Statement:**
I will maintain a cumulative GPA of at least 3.5 throughout my college career and graduate with a 3.5 GPA or higher.
**S — Specific:** What exactly will I do or achieve?
I will consistently prioritize my coursework, monitor my grades, and seek academic support when I am struggling in a class.

**M — Measurable:** How will I track progress? How will I know I succeeded?
I will check my cumulative GPA at the end of each semester and maintain a 3.5 or higher through graduation.

**A — Achievable:** Why is this realistic given my current situation?
Although maintaining a 3.5 GPA is challenging, I am already in my third year of college and have experience with college-level coursework. I can continue improving my study habits and use resources such as professors, tutoring, and academic advising when necessary.


**R — Relevant:** How does this connect to my POV Statement and career direction?
This goal supports my future career because a strong academic record can help me with internships, jobs, and other opportunities. It also reflects my POV Statement because it requires me to focus on steady progress rather than becoming overwhelmed by uncertainty about my future.

**T — Time-Bound:** By when will I achieve this? What is my deadline?
I will maintain a cumulative 3.5 GPA or higher through graduation.

**Immediate Next Action:** What is one concrete step I can take THIS WEEK toward this goal?
This week, I will check my current cumulative GPA and determine what semester GPA I need to maintain or reach a 3.5 by graduation.

### Goal 5 (Personal Development)

**Goal Statement:**
Throughout the Fall 2026 semester, I will prioritize my physical and mental well-being by moving my body for at least one hour a day on at least six days each week and practicing more positive self-talk.

**S — Specific:** What exactly will I do or achieve?
I will alternate between cardio, weights, walking, and yoga while also making a conscious effort to be kinder to myself when I make mistakes or feel uncertain.

**M — Measurable:** How will I track progress? How will I know I succeeded?
I will track my physical activity and aim for at least 6 active days per week. I will also reflect at least once a week on something I accomplished or handled well.

**A — Achievable:** Why is this realistic given my current situation?
I can adjust the type and intensity of activity based on my schedule while still maintaining consistency.

**R — Relevant:** How does this connect to my POV Statement and career direction?
This connects to my POV Statement because I tend to overthink, fear failure, and feel behind. Taking care of myself and recognizing my progress can help me develop confidence and become more comfortable with uncertainty.

**T — Time-Bound:** By when will I achieve this? What is my deadline?
I will follow this goal throughout the Fall 2026 semester and evaluate my progress at the end of the semester.

**Immediate Next Action:** What is one concrete step I can take THIS WEEK toward this goal?
This week, I will create a weekly schedule that includes one hour of movement each day and at least one rest/recovery period.
