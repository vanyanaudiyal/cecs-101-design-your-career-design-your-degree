# Career Vision Statement Activity

This activity guides you through a structured writing process to develop a meaningful Career Vision Statement. You will build it in layers — starting with raw material from your previous work, developing a draft, refining it, and then committing to a final version.

---

## Step 1 — Gather Your Raw Material

Before writing, pull together the following from your previous work:

- Your Personal Empathy Map insights
- Your SWOT Analysis
- Your Personal POV Statement
- Your RIASEC Profile
- Your Career Exploration Map

---

## Step 2 — Answer the Foundation Questions

What kind of work do I want to do?
I want to work with artificial intelligence and technology to solve real-world problems. I enjoy thinking creatively, learning how technology can improve people's lives, and developing ideas for tools or applications that can make things easier and more accessible. I want my work to involve innovation, problem-solving, and using AI in ways that have a practical purpose.
Who do I want to serve or impact?
I want to help people and communities who can benefit from better technology and easier access to useful resources. I am especially interested in creating solutions that can address large-scale problems and improve people's everyday lives. Ideally, my work would have an impact beyond just one company or one group of people.
What values do I want my career to embody?
I want my career to represent innovation, creativity, responsibility, empathy, and continuous learning. I want to use technology ethically and make sure the things I create are actually helpful to people. I also value being open-minded and willing to learn from challenges and mistakes.
What kind of professional do I want to become?
I want to become a creative, knowledgeable, and dependable professional who is not afraid to try new ideas. I want to be known as someone who can take a difficult problem, think of innovative solutions, and use technology to turn those ideas into something useful. I also want to be someone who works well with others and is willing to keep learning as technology changes.
What does success look like for me — not financially, but meaningfully?
Success would mean building or contributing to something that genuinely improves people's lives. I would feel successful if I could look at my work and know that it solved a real problem or made something better for other people. I also want to enjoy what I do, continue growing, and feel proud of the impact my career has had.

---

## Step 3 — Draft Your Career Vision Statement

Using your foundation questions as raw material, write a first draft of your Career Vision Statement. Aim for 3–5 sentences. Use the structure below as a guide — but feel free to depart from it once you have a draft:

> "My career vision is to [type of work] for/with [who you serve], so that [impact or outcome you want to create]. I want to be known as a professional who [qualities/values]. I see myself building a career in [field or sector] where I can [specific contribution], grounded in my commitment to [core value]."

**My Draft Career Vision Statement:**
My career vision is to work with artificial intelligence and technology to create innovative solutions to real-world problems and improve people's lives. I want to use my creativity, problem-solving skills, and interest in AI to develop technology that is useful, accessible, and meaningful. I want to be known as a professional who is innovative, responsible, hardworking, and always willing to learn. I see myself building a career in the AI and technology field where I can contribute to projects that have a positive impact on individuals and communities, while continuing to grow and adapt as technology evolves.

---

## Step 4 — Refine and Finalize

Read your draft aloud (or read it slowly in your head). Ask yourself:

- Does this sound like me, or does it sound like what I think I should say?
- Is it specific enough to be meaningful?
- Is it broad enough to allow for growth?
- Does it excite me when I read it?

Revise as needed and write your final version below.

**My Final Career Vision Statement:**

My career vision is to build a career in artificial intelligence and technology where I can use innovation and creativity to solve real-world problems and make people's lives better. I want to create or contribute to technology that is practical, accessible, and capable of making a meaningful difference in people's lives and communities. I am interested in solving a major world problem - hunger. I can combine my AI/computer science skills to help end world hunger. I want to be known as a creative, responsible, and determined professional who can turn ideas into useful solutions and who continues learning as technology changes. I want my career to be meaningful not only because of what I accomplish, but because of the positive impact my work has on others.
---

💡 **Note:** Your Career Vision Statement is a living document. You are not locked into it forever. You will revisit and revise it in Module 5 and beyond as your self-knowledge deepens and your career exploration continues.
