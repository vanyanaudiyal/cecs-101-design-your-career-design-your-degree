# RIASEC Profile Summary Activity

This activity walks you through taking the RIASEC assessment, interpreting your results, and connecting them to everything you already know about yourself from Module 2. By the end, you will have a complete **RIASEC Profile Summary** in your GitHub portfolio.

---

## Step 1 — Take the RIASEC Assessment

Complete the free RIASEC assessment using one of the following recommended tools:

- **O*NET Interest Profiler (Recommended)**
  Visit: [www.mynextmove.org/explore/ip](https://www.mynextmove.org/explore/ip)
  This is the official U.S. Department of Labor version — free, reliable, and directly linked to career data.

- **CareerOneStop Interest Assessment**
  Visit: [www.careeronestop.org/toolkit/careers/interest-assessment.aspx](https://www.careeronestop.org/toolkit/careers/interest-assessment.aspx)

Take the full assessment. Answer based on genuine interest, not perceived ability or what sounds impressive.

## Step 3 — Interpret Each of Your Top Three Types

For each of your top three RIASEC types, respond to the following prompts:

### My Top Three Types — Detailed Interpretation

Type 1: E — Enterprising

What this type typically values:
Enterprising people enjoy taking initiative, leading others, making decisions, persuading people, and working toward goals. They are often interested in business, leadership, entrepreneurship, management, and creating new opportunities. They tend to enjoy situations where they can influence others and turn ideas into action.

How this shows up in my life:
This type fits me because I enjoy taking ideas and thinking about how they could become something bigger. I am interested in technology and AI, but I am also interested in the business and leadership side of those areas. I like thinking about how technology can be used to solve real-world problems rather than only focusing on writing code. I also enjoy working with other people and taking initiative when I have an idea or a goal. I can see myself eventually being in a position where I help lead projects, make decisions, and turn new ideas into useful products or services.

Careers associated with this type that interest me:
Product Manager
Entrepreneur
Marketing Manager


---


Type 2: S — Social

What this type typically values:
Social people value helping, communicating with, teaching, supporting, and working with other people. They often enjoy teamwork, building relationships, understanding people's needs, and making a positive difference in other people's lives.

How this shows up in my life:
The Social type connects with my interest in solving problems that affect people. I enjoy working with others and communicating ideas rather than working completely independently. I am especially interested in using technology to make people's lives easier and address larger problems. For me, a successful career would not only be about making money but also about creating something useful and having a positive impact. I think understanding people and their needs is important when developing technology because a product is only useful if it actually helps the people who use it.

Careers associated with this type that interest me:
Human Resources Specialist
Training and Development Specialist
Community Outreach Coordinator

---

Type 3: I — Investigative

What this type typically values:
Investigative people value curiosity, research, problem-solving, analyzing information, and understanding how things work. They tend to enjoy asking questions, investigating problems, finding patterns, and using evidence to develop solutions.
How this shows up in my life:
The Investigative type connects with my interest in AI and technology because I enjoy learning how technology can be used to solve complicated problems. I like exploring new ideas and figuring out how different technologies could be applied to real-world situations. I am interested in AI because it combines technology, problem-solving, and innovation. Although I do not necessarily want a career that is focused entirely on coding, I still enjoy understanding technology and thinking about how it can be applied to create solutions.
Careers associated with this type that interest me:
AI/Product Manager
Market Research Analyst
UX Researcher

Where my Holland Code and Module 2 findings align:
My Holland Code of ESI (Enterprising, Social, Investigative) connects with many of the things I discovered about myself in Module 2. I am interested in technology and AI, but I am also interested in working with people, solving problems, and turning ideas into something useful. My Enterprising result connects with my interest in leadership, taking initiative, and developing ideas. My Social result connects with my interest in helping people and creating solutions that can have a positive impact. My Investigative result connects with my curiosity about AI, technology, and problem-solving.
Overall, my results show that I am not interested in technology simply for the technical side of it. I am more interested in using technology to solve problems, create opportunities, and help people.
Where there are surprises or tensions:
One surprise is that Enterprising was my highest interest, because I might have expected Investigative to be higher because of my interest in AI and technology. However, the results helped me realize that I may be more interested in the leadership, business, and application of technology than in spending all of my time doing technical work.
There is also a slight tension between my interest in technology and my preference for working with people. I enjoy learning about AI, but I do not necessarily want my entire career to involve coding. My RIASEC results helped me see that there are careers where I can combine technology with leadership, communication, business, and problem-solving.
What this tells me about my career direction:
The most important insight I gained from combining my RIASEC results with my Module 2 findings is that I do not have to choose between technology and working with people. My ESI profile suggests that I could be successful in a career where I understand technology and AI while also leading projects, communicating with people, and solving real-world problems.
Instead of focusing only on becoming a traditional programmer, I can explore careers such as AI product management, technology entrepreneurship, product strategy, technology consulting, or other roles that combine AI with business and leadership. This direction fits my interests because it allows me to be involved with emerging technology while also focusing on people, ideas, innovation, and meaningful problems.
